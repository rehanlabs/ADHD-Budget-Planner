📱 Step 1 & 2 — Add to Your Home Screen

Adding ADHD Budget Planner to your phone's home screen does three essential things:

  • Opens full-screen without browser URL bars, just like a native app store app.
  • Prevents Safari's 7-day auto-clear: Apple Safari auto-clears website data after 7 days of inactivity, but home-screen web apps are granted protected permanent storage!
  • Enables 100% offline mode: Launches and saves your data with zero cellular or Wi-Fi signal.

📱 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Name it ADHD Budget and tap Add.

🤖 Android (Google Chrome)

  1. Open the link above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) top-right.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
  4. Tap Install to add it to your apps list.
💻 Mac & Windows Users: Open in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon (computer with downward arrow) in the address bar. You can also run 100% offline directly from the offline-backup folder in this download!

🧠 Your 4 Neurodivergent Superpowers

Engineered from the ground up for dopamine-seeking, easily overwhelmed brains:

1. 🧮 Safe to Spend Today

No complex spreadsheets or 50 budget buckets. You get one simple number: what you can safely spend today without risking rent, bills, or debt payments. Before any purchase, ask: "Is this within today's safe number?"

2. ⏳ 48-Hour Impulse Vault

Suddenly want to buy something online? Don't fight the urge — park it in the Vault. A 48-hour calm countdown begins. Studies show over 80% of impulsive dopamine urges fade after 48 hours. If you still genuinely need it after the timer, buy it guilt-free!

3. 🧟 Zombie Mode

Experiencing ADHD burnout, executive dysfunction, or money anxiety? Tap Zombie Mode in the top navigation bar. Every chart, tab, and complex stat instantly disappears, leaving only your daily allowance and 1-tap quick spend buttons.

4. 📝 Brain Dump Scratchpad

Money anxiety often comes from fragmented thoughts ("need to cancel gym", "buy dog food Tuesday"). Use the Notes tab as a frictionless thought-dumping pad so nothing gets lost.

🔒 Protecting Your Privacy & Data

Your financial data is 100% private. It never touches a cloud server or third-party database. Because data lives on your device, we recommend:

  1. Go to Settings → Download Backup (.json) once a month and save it to your iCloud, Google Drive, or Downloads.
  2. If you ever get a new device, click Restore Backup to reload your entire planner in 2 seconds.
  3. Use QR Device Sync in Settings to transfer your budget between your computer and phone with zero internet!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this link to my bank account?
No. ADHD Budget Planner is 100% manual by design. Automatic bank syncs often break, disconnect, and trigger anxiety. Entering your balance takes 5 seconds and builds genuine financial mindfulness.
Q: Can I use different currencies?
Yes! Go to Settings to toggle between USD ($), CAD (CA$), GBP (£), EUR (€), AUD (AU$), and JPY (¥).
Q: Can I use this on both my phone and laptop?
Yes. You can use Settings → Download Backup on Device 1 and Restore on Device 2, or use the built-in QR Sync feature in Settings.
