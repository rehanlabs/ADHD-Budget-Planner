## Protecting Your Data (Do This Monthly)

Because all your data is stored locally, you should:

1. Go to **Settings → Download Backup (.json)** once a month
2. Save it to your **iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Downloads** folder
3. If you ever get a new phone, use **Restore Backup File** to bring everything back instantly

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my data disappear if I clear my browser?**
A: If you added the app to your Home Screen — no, it is protected. If you're using it in a browser tab on Safari without adding it to the home screen, Safari *can* clear data after 7 days of inactivity. Always take a monthly backup!

**Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?**
A: Yes! Use **Settings → Download Backup** on Device 1, then **Settings → Restore Backup** on Device 2. We also include a QR transfer tool (Settings → Send to Another Device) for one-tap cross-device sync without the internet.

**Q: Does this connect to my bank account?**
A: No. ADHD Budget Planner is completely manual (and that's by design!). You type in your balance, and the app does all the math. No bank login, no OAuth, no privacy risk.

**Q: What if I want to change currencies?**
A: Go to **Settings** and update your currency. Supports USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD, and JPY.

**Q: Can I share this with my partner?**
A: Your purchase is for personal use on your devices. If your partner needs their own copy, please purchase a second license from the Etsy listing. Thank you for supporting independent creators!
